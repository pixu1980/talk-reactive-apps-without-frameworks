What runs synchronously and what runs later?

One store write causes two kinds of work: immediate subscriber work during tickState.notify(), and microtask work through the scheduler.

Synchronous work

  • Model bindings subscribed to `tickState` resync controls
  • Computed signals become dirty and notify subscribers
  • DOM parts bound directly to computed signals may refresh immediately
  • Repeat parts bound to `visibleTodos` can reconcile blocks

Microtask work

  • Effects that read `tickState` are queued
  • Render effect calls `render(App(), root)`
  • Document preferences sync from store
  • This split keeps direct bindings responsive while batching top level effects
